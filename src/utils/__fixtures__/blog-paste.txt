Weeknight Chicken Tacos
Prep Time: 10 minutes
Cook Time: 15 minutes
Servings: 6

Ingredients
- 1 1/2 lbs chicken thighs
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp cumin

For the salsa:
- 3 tomatoes, diced
- 1/2 red onion

Instructions
1. Season the chicken with cumin and salt.
2. Heat the oil and cook the chicken for 6 minutes per side.
3. Slice and serve in warm tortillas with the salsa.

Notes
Leftovers keep for 3 days.
