Crispy Gnocchi With Burst Tomatoes and Mozzarella
By Ali Slagle
Updated Feb. 9, 2024
Total Time
25 minutes
Rating
4(8,512)
Notes
Read community notes
This one-skillet dinner of crispy gnocchi, jammy tomatoes and melty mozzarella is a play on pizza, and it comes together in about the time it takes to boil water.
Save
Print
INGREDIENTS
Yield:4 servings
1
(16- to 18-ounce) package shelf-stable or frozen gnocchi
¼
cup extra-virgin olive oil
2
pints cherry or grape tomatoes
4
garlic cloves, thinly sliced
Salt and black pepper
½
teaspoon red-pepper flakes
For serving
8
ounces fresh mozzarella, torn into bite-size pieces
Fresh basil leaves
Ingredient Substitution Guide
Nutritional analysis per serving (4 servings)
512 calories; 26 grams fat; 9 grams saturated fat
PREPARATION
Step 1
In a large (12-inch) nonstick skillet, heat 2 tablespoons oil over medium-high. Break apart any gnocchi that are stuck together, then add to the skillet in an even layer.
Step 2
Cook, covered and without stirring, until golden brown on the bottom, about 5 minutes.
Step 3
Add the tomatoes, garlic and remaining 2 tablespoons oil to the skillet; season with salt and pepper. Cook until the tomatoes burst.
Tip
Fresh gnocchi works too; it will brown faster.
